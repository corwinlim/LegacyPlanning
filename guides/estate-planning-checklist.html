Estate planning enquiry checklist: what should you prepare?

This guide is general information, not legal advice. Service eligibility, costs and formal applications must be verified independently. The enquiry preview is not accepting submissions.

What should you prepare before a first conversation?

Prepare a high-level description of your goals, family considerations, existing planning documents and categories of assets. You do not need to submit full identity numbers, bank account details or copies of legal documents to use this guide.

Which questions should you bring?

Ask which planning options might be relevant, who provides the formal service, how agent identity can be verified, what documents are eventually required, what the complete fees are and how appointments are confirmed.

How should you compare service providers?

Compare independently verifiable identity, written scope of service, transparent fees, appropriate qualifications and the official handling process. Avoid relying on pressure tactics or promises of guaranteed outcomes.

What happens after reading this checklist?

You may review the independent agent declaration and the enquiry preview. The preview form is disabled; no appointment is booked or confirmed through this website.
