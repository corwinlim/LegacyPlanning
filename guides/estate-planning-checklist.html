Estate Planning Consultation Checklist

A concise checklist to prepare useful questions before speaking with a qualified adviser.

Private educational preview only, not legal advice. No booking or application is accepted through this site.

Your preparation

List broad asset categories, existing wills or trust documents, dependants, overseas connections, and the decisions you would like help with.

Questions for the provider

Ask who provides the legal or trust service, what credentials apply, the scope and total fees, document execution, ongoing duties and dispute procedures.

Protect your information

Do not upload full identification documents, bank statements, passwords or sensitive financial information to a preview website. Verify the provider independently first.
