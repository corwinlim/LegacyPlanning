Will vs Trust in Malaysia: Questions to Ask

Understand the different purposes and practical considerations of wills and trusts.

Private educational preview only, not legal advice. No booking or application is accepted through this site.

What does a will do?

A will generally expresses wishes for the distribution of assets after death, subject to validity requirements and the applicable estate administration process.

What does a trust do?

A trust can involve a trustee holding or administering assets under specified terms for beneficiaries. Legal effect, costs and obligations depend on its structure.

What should I compare?

Ask about legal applicability, control, trustee duties, beneficiaries, set-up and ongoing costs, revocability and administration. Do not assume one option is always better.
